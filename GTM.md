# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy Blueprint

## 1. Exact Target User Profile
The exact target user is a Head of Engineering / Engineering Manager or VP of Finance at a Series A to Series B B2B SaaS startup with an engineering team size scaling between 15 to 60 operators. At this specific inflection point, individual engineers spin up self-serve licenses on individual corporate cards. Management has completely lost visibility into concurrent tooling overlaps.

## 2. Trigger Events: The Pre-Audit Lifecycle
Right before searching for or landing on this tool, the target profile is actively completing one of the following operations:
1. Reviewing the monthly corporate card statement and flagging an un-categorized surge under the line items Anthropic, OpenAI, or Github.
2. Preparing for a board of directors alignment deck where reducing technical infrastructure burn has been explicitly mandated.
3. Searching Google for queries like: "Cursor vs Github Copilot team pricing overlap", "manage multi-seat chatgpt enterprise subscription updates", or "how to cut startup SaaS burn without dropping velocity".

## 4. Zero-Dollar Distribution Loop: First 100 Users in 30 Days

### Days 1-10: The High-Waste Signal Scraping Loop (Target: 30 Users)
We will programmatically audit active startup team rosters on LinkedIn and GitHub matching the profile filter "Engineering Team Size: 20-50". We will isolate companies explicitly noting deep AI stacks in their job postings. We will cold-message the Engineering Manager via X/LinkedIn with a highly specific, zero-friction hook: "Hey [Name], noticed your team is scaling Cursor and Claude concurrently. 85% of startups matching your roster layout are leaking $600/mo on seat boundary overlaps. Dropped a 45-second interactive calculator to audit your stack without entering backend credentials."

### Days 11-20: The VC Portfolio Drop (Target: 50 Users)
We will target Principal/Portfolio Support operators at mid-tier VC funds. We present this tool as a value-add utility they can blast out to their portfolio founders to instantly protect seed runways. We offer them a custom portfolio link framing it as an exclusive efficiency initiative.

### Days 21-30: The Viral Link Loop (Target: 20 Users)
Leveraging the structural stripping of data inside SummaryPage.jsx, users are presented with a completely safe, un-identifiable public report link. When an engineering manager wants to prove to their VP of Finance that they need budget adjustments, they share this link. The footer contains a high-contrast tracking button: "Audit Your Own Stack via Credex", driving organic viral loops.

## 5. The Unfair Distribution Channel
The absolute unfair distribution advantage belongs to Credex. Because Credex sits on aggregated historical baseline data regarding enterprise cloud procurement thresholds, only Credex can cleanly say: "Your peer bracket is getting Claude enterprise credits at 35% below retail sheet cost." The audit math doesn't just calculate basic seat subtraction—it acts as a direct validation tool verifying that their configurations deviate from verified operational metrics.
