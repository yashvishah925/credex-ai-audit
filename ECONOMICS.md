# Financial Engineering and Unit Economics Model

## 1. Converted Lead Lifetime Value (LTV)

When a Series A startup books a consultation through this audit calculator framework, they enter the primary Credex enterprise optimization pipeline.

### LTV Calculation Rationale:
* Average Core SaaS Spend: A 40-person scaling engineering startup spends roughly $8,000/month across foundational cloud infrastructure, databases, and secondary AI sub-processors.
* Credex Fee Capture Structure: Credex operates on a 25% contingency margin against total validated annual recovery volume.
* Average Recovery Target: If the audit engine uncovers structural leakage of $600/month ($7,200 annualized), Credex captures a baseline optimization fee of $1,800 per conversion event.
* Upsell Pipeline Valuation: 30% of validated leads convert upward into deep multi-year enterprise contract optimization programs, pushing the macro average Lead Lifetime Value (LTV) to $4,500.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Modeling

Because the GTM plan leverages direct organic distribution, our primary cost boundaries sit entirely inside temporal execution and computation hours.

* Direct Founder Outreach: 15 minutes of asynchronous DM copywriting per qualified outbound lead. Assumes a 10% translation loop efficiency. Estimated CAC: $45.00
* VC Portfolio Blast: Zero execution friction. Initial relationship management setup requires ~4 hours of direct outreach. Estimated CAC: $8.00
* Organic Share Loops: Completely automated client-to-client link forwarding loops managed via Supabase. Estimated CAC: $0.05

## 3. Funnel Conversion & Profitability Benchmarks

To cover standard serverless operations, database persistence layers, and token generation fees, the conversion pipeline must maintain these strict tracking thresholds:

* Audit Complete -> Consultation Booked Rate: 12% minimum for high-waste pools (Savings >= $500/mo).
* Consultation Booked -> Retained Contract Close Rate: 15% baseline close rate.
* Breakeven Verification: With an LTV of $4,500 against a blended programmatic acquisition cost profile of under $50, the framework operates at an extreme 90x LTV-to-CAC safety ratio, maintaining strong cash profitability from day one.

## 4. The Path to $1,000,000 ARR in 18 Months

To drive $1M Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) within the 18-month execution window, the infrastructure must scale to the following target volume:

* Total Annual Billing Target: $1,000,000 ARR / $4,500 average converted LTV = 222 completely closed conversion contracts per year.
* Required Inbound Audits: Scaling backward through our 1.8% end-to-end close rate macro vector, the framework needs to process 12,333 total completed audits annually.
* Daily Run-Rate Metric: This scales down to exactly 34 unique completed audits per day. This is an exceptionally achievable volume that easily fits within the infrastructure's 10k requests/day high-throughput architecture specification.
